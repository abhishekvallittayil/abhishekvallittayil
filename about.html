About Me

My interest in IT and technology began in 2019 during a college workshop. During the COVID-19 lockdown in 2020, I dedicated myself to mastering Linux and cybersecurity — building a strong self-taught foundation on top of my formal education.

My hands-on experience spans Windows Server 2022, Active Directory, Group Policy, VPNs, IIS, network hardening, and penetration testing.

Currently collaborating on DeepStride (wearable health technology & security testing), preparing for my CCNA, and seeking roles in Cybersecurity, IT Support, or System Administration across Canada. I thrive on understanding how systems break — so I can make them harder to break.
